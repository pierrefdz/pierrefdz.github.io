## About me

<div class="row">
  <div class="col-md-8" markdown="1">
  I am a Research Scientist at FAIR Paris (Fundamental AI Research lab at Meta).
  
  My research focuses on content protection and safety in machine learning, with a particular interest in watermarking generative models. My goal is to make content generated by AI traceable and identifiable. I also study how watermarks can reveal [which data a model was trained on](/publications/radioactive/).
  I notably developed:
  - [TextSeal](/publications/textseal/), a state-of-the-art watermark for LLMs, with stronger detection than SynthID-text, localization in mixed human/AI documents, and no inference overhead or quality loss (NeurIPS 2026, co-led with [Tom Sander](https://sandertom.github.io/));
  - the invisible watermarking of Meta's AI-generated images, from Imagine to [Muse Image](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-image-muse-video-msl/), with a public detection tool at [meta.ai/identification](https://meta.ai/identification);
  - [Video Seal](/publications/videoseal/) and [Pixel Seal](/publications/pixelseal/), state-of-the-art open-source image and video watermarking models, now part of [Content Seal](https://facebookresearch.github.io/content-seal/);
  - [AudioSeal](/publications/audioseal/), the audio watermarking algorithm used in public research demos from FAIR, such as [Audiobox](https://audiobox.metademolab.com) and [Seamless](https://seamless.metademolab.com), serving 100k users daily (joint work with [Hady Elsahar](https://www.hadyelsahar.io/));
  - [Stable Signature](https://ai.meta.com/blog/stable-signature-watermarking-generative-ai/), a method to watermark generative models even if they are open-sourced.
  {: style="font-size: 0.9rem;"}

  I also co-organized the [GenAI Watermarking workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/genai-watermark/) at ICLR 2025 and a [tutorial on watermarking](https://vishal3477.github.io/watermarking-cvpr2026/) at CVPR 2026, and I am regularly consulted on the technical aspects of AI transparency regulations, such as the EU AI Act.

  I did my PhD at FAIR and Inria Rennes, advised by [Matthijs Douze](https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=0eFZtREAAAAJ&hl=fr), [Hervé Jégou](https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=1lcY2z4AAAAJ&hl=fr) and [Teddy Furon](https://scholar.google.fr/citations?user=aLUbWzAAAAAJ&hl=fr). 
  Prior to my PhD I studied at [École polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu/en), majoring in computer science and mathematics for visual computing. I also hold a Master's degree from [École des Ponts ](https://www.ecoledesponts.fr/en) and from [Paris-Saclay University](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en) where I studied Mathematics for vision and learning.
